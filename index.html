How we work

The UMBRA™ approach

Unlike dev shops or agencies, we operate as a founder-first innovation partner, from defining the right problem through working code, go-to-market and scaling your team.

Phase 1

Understand

Market research to identify ideal user and customer personas based on your industry or domain expertise.

Phase 2

Mobilize

Plan product initiatives that bridge the gap between industry needs and product requirements.

Phase 3

Build

Design technology platforms that turn requirements into functional, cost-effective prototypes.

Phase 4

Reflect and Adapt

Capture product analytics and metrics that show what is working, so each new offering builds on the last.

UMBRA™ is an acronym for the key phases of managing any innovation project.
